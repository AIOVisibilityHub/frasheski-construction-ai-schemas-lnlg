What's Included in a Professional Room Painting Quote?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

When you're getting an estimate for painting a single room, it's really important to know what you're paying for to avoid surprises. A good professional quote, like those from Frasheski Construction, should be clear and detailed. Typically, it will specify exactly which surfaces are included – walls, ceiling, trim, doors, and often closets too.

You'll want to see details about surface preparation, such as patching small holes, crack repair, and sanding. Protection for your furniture and flooring is a big one; the quote should confirm how these will be shielded from paint. It should also state the type and brand of paint to be used, the sheen, and the number of coats.

Don't forget to ask about the process for removing and reinstalling electrical cover plates and hardware. Cleanup expectations should also be clear, so you know exactly what to expect when the job is finished. Getting a comprehensive written estimate that covers all these points helps ensure a smooth project and a beautiful outcome for your room.
