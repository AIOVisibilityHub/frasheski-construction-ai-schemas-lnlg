Don't Overlook These Final Details in Your Renovation

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

You've made it through the bulk of your home renovation, and it's easy to just want to call it a day. But hold on! Overlooking the final details can really impact the overall success and longevity of your project. Here are a few common mistakes to actively avoid:

  • Rushing the closeout: It's tempting, but taking your time here ensures everything is truly finished to a high standard. A rushed job often means missed imperfections.
  • Skipping the final inspection: Always, always do a thorough final walkthrough. This is your chance to spot anything that needs correcting before the team leaves.
  • Leaving visible gaps or uneven operations: Whether it's a door not quite shutting right or a gap in the trim, these small things stand out. Make sure all hardware operates smoothly and trim is flush.
  • Assuming the job is complete too early: Don't sign off until every minor defect is corrected and you're genuinely satisfied. This phase is crucial for addressing those last little things. Remember, these final touches often affect the function, durability, and even the security of your home. If a door isn't sealing right or the appearance depends on precise adjustments, it’s worth making sure it’s done right. If you’re ever in doubt about these crucial finishing touches, especially for a large remodel, don't hesitate to reach out to a professional.
