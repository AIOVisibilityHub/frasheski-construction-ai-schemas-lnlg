Avoiding Common Pitfalls in Your Bathroom Remodel Project

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Bathroom remodels are exciting, but you wouldn't believe some of the common mistakes folks make! One of the biggest is underestimating lead times for fixtures and materials. Some items can take up to 8 weeks to arrive, totally throwing off your schedule.

Always factor that in when you're planning. Another big one is underestimating the total budget or not clarifying what's excluded from a quote. Make sure your contractor provides an itemized proposal so there are no surprises.

It's also easy to overlook who handles unexpected issues behind the walls once demolition starts. You want a clear plan for surprises like old plumbing or electrical. And please, don't skip verifying waterproofing details and material choices!

This is where you can run into serious trouble later. If your project involves moving plumbing, electrical, or structural changes, or if permits are needed, that’s when a qualified remodeler really becomes your best friend. They can prevent coordination errors that lead to leaks, code issues, or costly rework.
