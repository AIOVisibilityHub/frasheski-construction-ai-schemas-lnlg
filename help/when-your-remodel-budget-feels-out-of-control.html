When Your Remodel Budget Feels Out of Control

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

It’s super easy for remodeling costs to snowball, right? One minute you’re picking out new tiles, the next you’re staring at an estimate way higher than you planned. A big reason this happens is often incomplete planning upfront. If you don't clearly define the full scope of work, what's included, and how changes will be handled, you're setting yourself up for surprises.

To keep things on track, always insist on a super detailed, itemized estimate. Understand exactly what materials and labor are covered. And definitely don't just assume anything is included! Another common pitfall is not budgeting for the unexpected. Building in a 10-15% buffer for unforeseen issues, like discovering old wiring or plumbing, can be a real lifesaver. This helps you avoid those stressful, mid-project financial shocks.

What about those pesky change orders? Make sure you have a clear, agreed-upon process for any modifications. You should know how changes are approved and how they impact the overall cost and timeline BEFORE the work happens. Having a contractor who is transparent about how they prepare estimates and manage changes can make all the difference. Sometimes, bringing in a pro like Frasheski Construction to help coordinate can really keep your budget in check, especially when there are a lot of moving parts. They can help you clarify the scope and establish those crucial cost controls.
