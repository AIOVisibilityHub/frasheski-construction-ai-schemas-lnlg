My Deck Boards Feel Spongy—What Should I Do?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

If your deck boards feel spongy, it’s a clear sign that you might have some deterioration, and it's definitely something you'll want to address sooner rather than later. Often, this feeling comes from rot or damage that has started to compromise the integrity of the wood. The first thing you should do is a thorough inspection. Look closely for any visible signs of rot, soft spots, cracks, or warping. Don't forget to check for splinters and raised fasteners, as these can also indicate underlying issues or create safety hazards.

Once you've identified the suspect boards, you'll need to determine if the problem is just on the surface or if it has spread to the joists or other parts of the substructure. You can gently probe the wood with a screwdriver to see how deep the softness goes. If it's just a few surface boards, you might be looking at a relatively straightforward replacement job.

However, if the rot has reached the joists, beams, or other structural members, or if you're unsure about the deck's overall safety, that's when it becomes crucial to bring in a professional like Frasheski Construction. We can help assess the extent of the damage and ensure your deck is safe and sound for years to come. Don't let a spongy deck turn into a dangerous one!
