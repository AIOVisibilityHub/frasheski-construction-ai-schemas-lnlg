Mistakes to Steer Clear Of During Your Tile Project

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Embarking on a tile project can be exciting, but there are some common mistakes that can turn your dream bathroom into a headache. One of the biggest blunders is poor surface preparation. You can't just slap new tile over an uneven or dirty surface; it'll lead to cracking and an unprofessional look.

Another crucial mistake is skipping waterproofing where it's absolutely necessary, especially in showers or around tubs. This can lead to leaks and expensive water damage down the road. Using the wrong type of underlayment or adhesive for your specific tiles and substrate is another pitfall, often resulting in tiles coming loose or an uneven finish.

Don't forget about uneven layouts, where tiles aren't properly spaced or aligned, making the whole room look off. Finally, neglecting to account for transitions between different flooring types, proper drainage slopes, or essential movement joints can cause long-term issues. To avoid these headaches, take your time with prep, always waterproof where needed, and choose the right materials.
