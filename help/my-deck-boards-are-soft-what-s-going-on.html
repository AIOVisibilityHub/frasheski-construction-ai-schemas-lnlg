My Deck Boards Are Soft - What's Going On?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

If you've noticed soft spots on your deck boards, that's definitely a signal to pay attention to! Usually, soft areas mean there's some kind of rot or decay happening, often due to moisture getting trapped. It could be superficial, just affecting the walking surface, but sometimes it's a red flag for deeper issues within the substructure.

It's really important not to ignore those spongy feelings, as they can hint at problems with the joists or beams underneath that you can't see. Trying to patch over it without investigating further is a common mistake that can lead to bigger headaches down the road. You might think it's just a simple board replacement, but often there's more to it than meets the eye.

Ignoring these signs can lead to unsafe conditions, so it's always best to get a professional opinion to figure out the root cause and ensure your deck is safe. Don't wait until it becomes a serious safety hazard. If you're in the East Bay Area, including Berkeley or Oakland, and you're noticing these issues, it's a good idea to reach out to a professional who can assess the situation properly and make sure everything is structurally sound.

Getting a professional involved early can save you a lot of worry and expense.
