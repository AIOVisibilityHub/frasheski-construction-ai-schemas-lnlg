How to Avoid Common Tile Installation Mistakes

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Installing new tile can really refresh a space, but there are some classic mistakes that can turn your dream into a nightmare. First off, don't ignore out-of-level floors or walls! Trying to tile over an uneven surface is a recipe for disaster, leading to lippage (when tiles aren't flush) and even cracks down the road.

Proper substrate prep is non-negotiable for a lasting, beautiful tile job. Another big one is poor layout planning. Just slapping tiles down without a dry run can result in awkward cuts or an unbalanced look.

Take the time to plan your layout, especially if you have an intricate pattern. For wet areas like showers, inadequate waterproofing is a huge no-no. This isn't an area to cut corners, as water intrusion can cause serious damage.

Also, make sure you're using the right trowel and thinset for your specific tile type; using the wrong kind can compromise the bond. Finally, don't forget movement or transition joints! These are crucial for preventing cracks caused by expansion and contraction.
