My Fence is Leaning: Repair or Replace?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

It's a common dilemma in the East Bay: you look out at your yard and notice your fence isn't standing quite as straight as it used to. When your fence starts to lean, the big question is always, "Can I fix this, or do I need a whole new fence?" Well, it really depends on what's causing the lean.

Sometimes, it's just one or two posts that have gone rogue, maybe due to rot or some loose footing. Other times, soil movement in our lovely Berkeley and Oakland areas can cause broader issues. If it's just an isolated problem, like a single bad post, a repair can definitely save the day.

You might just need to re-set it or replace that specific post. But if you're seeing widespread lean, multiple failing posts, or if more than about a quarter of your fence looks compromised, then you're probably looking at a replacement. Trying to "save" a rotten post is often a waste of time and money, so really assessing the damage is key.

When in doubt, especially if the lean is significant or impacts your property line, it's smart to get a professional opinion. We’re here to help you figure out the best, most cost-effective solution for your unique situation.
