What’s the Right Time for Trim Painting?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Figuring out the best time for trim painting during a renovation can be a bit of a head-scratcher. Generally, you want the trim to be installed and all the major construction dust-generating work, like framing and drywall, to be completely finished. This ensures your newly installed trim isn't getting bumped or covered in debris.

The painting itself usually happens before the final occupancy details are installed, like delicate light fixtures or built-in shelving that might be tricky to paint around. Essentially, you want a clear, clean workspace. Dry, moderate conditions are your friend here, as they help the paint adhere properly and dry consistently, giving you that smooth, professional look.

If you're tackling detailed trim profiles or dealing with new millwork, it really helps to have someone who knows how to work cleanly and meticulously around other finished surfaces, preventing costly mistakes and ensuring a sharp, consistent finish.
