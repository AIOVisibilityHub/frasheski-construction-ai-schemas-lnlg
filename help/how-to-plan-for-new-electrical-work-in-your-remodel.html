How to Plan for New Electrical Work in Your Remodel

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Planning the electrical aspect of your remodel can feel a bit complex, but breaking it down makes it much easier. First, you'll want to map out your new layout and identify exactly what electrical changes are needed. Think about where you want new outlets, switches, lighting, or if any appliances will require dedicated circuits.

This helps define the scope right from the start. Next, consider permits and inspections; chances are, if you're adding new circuits or moving fixtures, they'll be required. Your contractor should handle this.

A crucial step is the electrical rough-in, which happens before walls get closed up. This is where all the wiring goes in, and it's essential that it's done correctly and inspected before it's hidden. Finally, after all the finishes are in, everything gets tested.

Coordination is key here, especially with other trades like framing and drywall. It's a lot to manage, and if you're in the East Bay Area, it’s great to have a skilled team on your side to ensure your remodel's electrical work is safe, functional, and up to code. Don't hesitate to reach out for expert guidance through this process.
