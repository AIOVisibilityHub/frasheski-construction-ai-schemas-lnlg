What to Expect When Painting Your Kitchen Cabinets

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Thinking about giving your kitchen cabinets a fresh look? It's more than just slapping on a new coat of paint! A professional approach usually starts with thorough cleaning and degreasing to ensure the new paint adheres properly.

Then comes the sanding or scuffing, which is crucial for creating a smooth, durable surface. Any dings or imperfections get repaired and filled before meticulous dust removal and masking protect your kitchen. Once that's all done, a quality primer goes on, followed by several finish coats.

Often, cabinet doors and drawers are handled separately from the box frames to ensure an even, factory-like finish. Finally, everything's reassembled, and you'll get a walkthrough to make sure you're thrilled with the result. This detailed process ensures a long-lasting, beautiful finish that really transforms your kitchen.

If you're looking for a smooth, durable finish without the hassle, getting an expert involved is often the best route.
