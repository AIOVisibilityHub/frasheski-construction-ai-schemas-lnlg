What Happens During a Professional Door Hardware Install?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

So, you're wondering what a professional door and hardware installation actually looks like? Well, it kicks off with a super important step: a site assessment and precise measurements. This ensures everything from the door alignment to the handing is just right for your space.

We'll check if your chosen hardware is compatible and then carefully install the door and its components. This isn't just about screwing things in; it’s about making sure everything functions perfectly. We'll test the latch, check how it closes, and ensure there’s even spacing around the door slab.

Most standard installations take a few hours, but sometimes, if your home is a bit older or the framing isn't perfectly square, it can take a little longer. It's all part of the process to get you a door that not only looks great but works flawlessly. If you're in the East Bay Area and need a hand with door and hardware installation, feel free to reach out.

We ensure a smooth process from start to finish.
