Mistakes to Avoid: Door and Hardware Installation

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Nobody wants a door that sticks or a lock that won't turn, right? When it comes to door and hardware installation, there are definitely some common pitfalls to sidestep.

  • Ordering the wrong hardware: This happens more often than you'd think! Make sure your chosen hardware matches your door's prep and handing to avoid a headache. Always double-check those specifications.
  • Skipping the dry-fit: Don't just jump right into attaching hinges and strike plates. Dry-fitting them first lets you see if everything aligns properly before you make permanent changes.
  • Ignoring uneven gaps: After installation, check the spacing around your door slab. Uneven gaps can lead to drafts and an ill-fitting door. Small adjustments can make a big difference.
  • Not checking latch and deadbolt function: A door isn't properly installed until the latch moves smoothly and the deadbolt engages securely. Test it multiple times!
  • Assuming the framing is perfect: Especially in older homes, your door opening might not be perfectly square. Professionals know how to work around this, but DIYers can get into trouble. If you're unsure, getting some expert help can save you a lot of time and frustration in the long run. When the opening is out of square or the installation affects exterior weather sealing and security, having a qualified professional step in is always a good idea. They can ensure your door and hardware will align and function correctly for years to come, especially in areas like Berkeley and the wider East Bay.
