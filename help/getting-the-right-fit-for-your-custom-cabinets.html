Getting the Right Fit for Your Custom Cabinets

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Getting custom-fit cabinets installed in your home is a multi-step process, but understanding each phase can help you feel more confident and prepared. From the initial spark of an idea to the final reveal, here’s a typical timeline you can expect.

Phase 1: Initial Consultation and Vision (Week 1)

This is where your ideas start to take shape! You'll meet with a cabinet professional, like Frasheski Construction, to discuss your needs, style preferences, and budget. This isn't just about aesthetics; it's about how you use your space in places like Berkeley or Alameda. Be ready to talk about your daily routines, what you store, and any pain points with your current setup. This helps define the project's scope.

Phase 2: Site Measurement and Design (Weeks 2-3)

Once your vision is clear, the contractor will take precise measurements of your space. This is critical for custom cabinets, ensuring everything fits perfectly – especially in older homes in the East Bay area that might have unique dimensions. Based on these measurements and your consultation, detailed design and shop drawings will be created. You'll review these carefully, making any adjustments until you approve the final layout, materials, and hardware. Don't be afraid to ask questions during this phase; it's your chance to ensure every detail is exactly right.

Phase 3: Fabrication and Production (Weeks 4-10)

With approved designs, your custom cabinets head into the fabrication stage. This is where skilled craftsmen build your cabinets to specification, often using high-quality materials and precise techniques. The timing here can vary quite a bit, typically ranging from 4 to 12 weeks, depending on the complexity of your design, the materials chosen, and the workshop's current schedule. Patience is key during this phase, as quality custom work takes time.

Phase 4: Installation and Final Touches (Weeks 11-12)

Once your cabinets are ready, it’s time for installation! The team will carefully transport and install your new cabinetry, ensuring everything is level, plumb, and securely fastened. This includes installing doors, drawers, hardware, and any special features you’ve chosen.

After installation, there will be a final walkthrough to check for any adjustments or touch-ups needed. This is where your dream kitchen or bathroom truly comes to life. Remember, a single, dedicated contractor like Frasheski Construction ensures seamless accountability throughout this entire process, making sure your project in Kensington or Sausalito is completed to your satisfaction.
