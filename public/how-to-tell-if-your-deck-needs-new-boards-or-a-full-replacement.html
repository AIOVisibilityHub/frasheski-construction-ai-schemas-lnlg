How to Tell if Your Deck Needs New Boards or a Full Replacement

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

It's not always obvious whether your deck just needs a few new boards or if you're looking at a more extensive repair. The key is to assess the extent of the damage. Start by walking around your deck and really paying attention to what you feel and see. Are there just a few isolated soft spots, or does a large section feel weak? Look for specific issues like rot, noticeable cracks, splintering, or boards that are clearly warped or bowing. Raised fasteners can also be a clue that the wood is deteriorating around them.

If the issues are contained to just a handful of surface boards, and the underlying joists and substructure feel solid and look free of rot, then a simple deck board replacement or “resurfacing” might be all you need. This involves carefully removing the damaged boards, preparing the joists, and installing new, matching decking. However, if you find that the rot has spread to the joists, beams, or other critical structural components, or if there's significant movement in multiple areas, you might be facing a larger repair that goes beyond just the surface.

In those cases, it's really important to get a professional evaluation. A company like Frasheski Construction can inspect your entire deck, identify any deeper structural issues, and help you decide the best course of action to ensure your deck is safe and lasts.
