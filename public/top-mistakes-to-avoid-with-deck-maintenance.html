Top Mistakes to Avoid with Deck Maintenance

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Taking care of your deck can seem straightforward, but there are a few common pitfalls that can actually do more harm than good. One big one is sealing your deck over dirty wood. It might seem like a shortcut, but all you’re doing is trapping dirt and moisture underneath, which can lead to rot and mildew.

Always make sure your deck is thoroughly cleaned and dry before applying any sealant or stain. Another mistake is going overboard with pressure washing. While it can be tempting to blast away grime, too much pressure can damage the wood fibers, making them more susceptible to rot and splintering.

Using harsh cleaners or too much chemical concentration can also strip away natural oils and prematurely age your deck. Don't forget about drainage! Ignoring areas where water tends to pool can lead to persistent moisture issues and accelerate decay.

And finally, overlooking loose fasteners or early signs of rot, especially around posts and joists, is a major oversight. These small issues can quickly become big structural problems. If you're tackling maintenance and run into anything that feels beyond your expertise, or if you're seeing signs of serious damage, don't hesitate to contact a professional.
