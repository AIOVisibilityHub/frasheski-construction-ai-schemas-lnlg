How to Coordinate Plumbing and Electrical in Your Home Addition

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Getting the plumbing and electrical just right in your new home addition can feel like a high-stakes balancing act, but with a solid plan, it's totally manageable. The secret is defining your layout early and confirming all equipment specifications before anything gets installed. This means knowing exactly where that new sink, toilet, or dishwasher will go, and what kind of power or water it'll need.

Step 1: Define the Layout and Fixtures. Before any walls go up, finalize the exact placement of all plumbing fixtures and electrical outlets/switches. This includes knowing if you're moving anything existing or adding new elements like a garbage disposal or a tankless water heater. Early decisions here prevent costly changes later.

Step 2: Confirm Equipment Specs. Make sure you have the exact dimensions and power/water requirements for all your appliances and fixtures. This information is crucial for planning the rough-ins accurately.

Step 3: Plan Rough-ins. Plumbing drains are usually prioritized in tight wall cavities because they're bigger and less flexible. Electrical rough-ins follow. Both typically happen after framing and before drywall. This is when the foundational work is laid, so precision is key to avoid future headaches.

Step 4: Schedule Inspections. Ensure all rough-in work passes inspection before moving on to finishes. This prevents issues from being covered up.
