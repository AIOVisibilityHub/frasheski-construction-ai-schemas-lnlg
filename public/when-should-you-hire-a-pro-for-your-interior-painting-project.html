When Should You Hire a Pro for Your Interior Painting Project?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Thinking about a fresh coat of paint for your interiors? It's a great way to update your home! But when does it make sense to call in the pros instead of tackling it yourself?

Well, if you're dealing with occupied spaces, meaning you're still living in the home while the work is happening, a professional can really minimize disruption and coordinate things efficiently. For larger or multi-room jobs, the sheer scale of the work can be overwhelming for a DIYer. Professionals have the team and equipment to get it done faster and more efficiently.

Tight timelines are another big indicator; if you need the work completed quickly, a pro can usually deliver. And if your walls need surface repairs before painting – think patching holes, sanding, or dealing with water damage – a professional painter has the expertise to handle these prep steps correctly, ensuring a smooth, flawless finish. Finally, if you have high expectations for the finish quality, or if the project requires coordination with other trades (like in a full kitchen or bathroom remodel), hiring a qualified professional like Frasheski Construction in Berkeley means you'll get a top-notch result without the headaches of doing it yourself.
