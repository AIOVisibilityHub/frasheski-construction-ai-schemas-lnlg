How to Plan Your Custom Kitchen Cabinet Project

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Planning a custom kitchen cabinet project? That's an exciting undertaking that can totally transform your space! To make sure it goes smoothly, there are a few key steps you'll want to follow. First off, you need to clearly define what "upgrade" means for you. Are we talking about new doors and drawer fronts, or a complete overhaul with new cabinet boxes? Think about the materials, finishes, and the overall style you're aiming for. This initial vision is crucial.

Next, verify the construction and materials of the cabinets you're considering. Don't just look at the pretty facade! Ask about the quality of the cabinet boxes, the drawer construction, and the hardware.

Decide if you prefer framed or frameless cabinets and what overlay style best suits your aesthetic. Pay close attention to what storage accessories are included, or what can be added. This is also the time to review any shop drawings or design details with your professional before anything is ordered.

Custom projects can take some time; a full custom kitchen might take 14 to 20 weeks from start to finish, with production lead times often 8 to 14 weeks after you approve the design. Hiring a qualified professional is super important here, especially if you're looking for custom sizing or an altered layout.
