Why Does My New Room Construction Project Always Seem to Go Over Budget?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

It's frustrating when a new room construction project starts strong and then, seemingly out of nowhere, costs begin to escalate. One major culprit is making design changes too late in the game. Once demolition or site prep has begun, altering the plan can lead to significant rework and material changes, which naturally drive up expenses.

Another common issue is underestimating the lead times for permits and materials. If you're rushing to get something done, you might pay a premium for expedited services or specific materials, or even worse, face project delays while waiting for items to arrive. A vague scope of work at the outset is also a big problem.

If you haven't clearly defined every aspect of what's included, it's easy for 'extras' to creep in, each adding to the final bill.

To keep your budget in check, start by thoroughly documenting your scope and any changes. This means getting everything in writing, from initial designs to every alteration. Next, plan for permit and material lead times early on.

Don't assume everything will be immediately available. Research local Berkeley and East Bay regulations and supplier schedules. Finally, work with a professional from the beginning to get an itemized estimate.

They can help you identify potential cost drivers upfront and build in a contingency budget for the unexpected. Frasheski Construction can help you map out these details to avoid those common budget surprises.
