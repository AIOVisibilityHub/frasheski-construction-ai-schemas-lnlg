What to Ask Before Hiring for Whole-Home Painting

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Thinking about a fresh coat for your entire home's interior? That's a big project, and you want to make sure it's done right. Before you commit to a painting contractor, it's really smart to have a clear understanding of what's included and how the process will unfold.

You'll want to ask about the exact scope—which rooms and surfaces like walls, trim, doors, and ceilings are covered. Don't forget to clarify any exclusions too! It’s also important to understand the specific paint products and grades they plan to use, and how many coats will be applied.

A written quote detailing all of this is super important. Make sure you get a clear timeline with start and finish dates, especially if you have an event coming up or need access to certain rooms at specific times. Professionals often assist with color selection, which can be a huge help when you're coordinating multiple rooms.

This ensures you're on the same page and helps avoid any surprises. Remember, for a project this size, a detailed plan from an experienced team like Frasheski Construction can make all the difference.
