How to Ensure Your New Shower or Tub Installation Goes Smoothly

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Getting a new shower or tub installed can really elevate your bathroom, and a smooth process means less stress for you. Here's a simple checklist to make sure you're on the right track:

  • Confirm Dimensions and Condition: Before buying anything, double-check the existing tub/shower dimensions and assess its current condition. This prevents headaches later.
  • Choose Wisely: Select your upgrade type and materials carefully. Think about product durability, ease of cleaning, and how it fits with your overall bathroom style.
  • Check Drainage and Waterproofing: This is super important! Ensure that the drainage system is adequate and that all waterproofing details are planned and understood.
  • Schedule Installation: Coordinate with your installer, keeping in mind that product availability and team schedules can affect start dates. Be aware that permits might be needed if plumbing or electrical changes are involved.
  • Verify Before Completion: Once installed, thoroughly check the fit, sealing, and finish. Make sure everything is perfectly in place and watertight before giving the final sign-off.

Following these steps can prevent common mistakes and ensure a beautiful, functional result.
