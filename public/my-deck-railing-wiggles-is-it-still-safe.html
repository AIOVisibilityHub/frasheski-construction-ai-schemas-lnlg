My Deck Railing Wiggles—Is It Still Safe?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

A wobbly deck railing is more than just an annoyance; it's a serious safety concern that you shouldn't ignore. When your railing moves, it indicates that something isn't properly secured, and it could fail when leaned upon, leading to a fall hazard. This is especially critical for elevated decks. The first thing to do is determine exactly why it's wiggling. Is it a loose handrail, a wobbling post, or damaged balusters (pickets)? Sometimes, the problem lies with corrosion or rot where the connections are made.

Start by testing each section of the railing. Give it a good push and pull to identify where the looseness originates. Then, inspect where the posts connect to the deck itself.

This is a common failure point. Look for loosened fasteners, rusted hardware, or any signs of deteriorated wood or metal. It's easy to just focus on the visible top rail, but the real danger often lurks in the attachment points below.

If you're dealing with a railing on an elevated deck and the posts are loose at the connection, or if you see significant wood rot or corrosion at these structural points, it's definitely time to call in a professional.
