How Long Does Professional Kitchen Cabinet Painting Really Take?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

It's easy to think painting kitchen cabinets is a quick weekend project, but if you want a professional, durable finish, it typically takes several business days. For a small kitchen, you're usually looking at about 4–6 days. A medium-sized kitchen might stretch to 6–8 days, and for a larger kitchen, it could be 8–10 business days.

This isn't just about applying paint; it accounts for all the crucial steps like thorough cleaning, sanding, repairs, and most importantly, adequate drying and curing time between coats. Rushing these stages, especially drying and curing, is a common mistake that can lead to chipping or marks down the line. A pro knows to factor in that vital time for the paint to fully harden, ensuring your cabinets look great and stand up to daily use.

For an accurate timeline and a lasting finish, consider working with someone who understands these nuances.
