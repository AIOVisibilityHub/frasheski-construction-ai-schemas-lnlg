Painting Perfection: What to Do Before the Brushes Come Out

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Getting ready for interior painting might seem simple, but a little prep work goes a long way to ensure a beautiful finish. First things first, you'll want to confirm the exact rooms and surfaces that are getting painted. This includes walls, ceilings, trim – everything!

Next, take a good look at the wall and ceiling condition. Are there any cracks, holes, or imperfections that need to be patched or sanded? Addressing these now will make a huge difference in the final look.

Then, it's all about prepping and protecting adjacent areas. This means moving furniture, covering floors and fixtures, and taping off trim you don't want painted. You might be surprised how much dust and splatter can happen!

Coordinating access and sequencing around other trades or if you'll be occupying the space is also crucial. You don't want painters and electricians tripping over each other. Finally, and this is super important, verify your color and material choices before any paint goes on the wall.

A small sample can look very different once it's covering a whole room. For larger projects or if you're unsure about the best way to prep, getting professional help from someone like Frasheski Construction can really make a difference.
