When Should You Upgrade Your Kitchen Plumbing During a Remodel?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Deciding when to upgrade your kitchen plumbing during a remodel can feel a bit overwhelming, but it boils down to a few key scenarios. You should definitely consider an upgrade if you’re planning to relocate your sink or any major appliances like a dishwasher or refrigerator with a water line. Moving these means moving pipes, and that's a job for the pros.

Another strong indicator is if you're replacing old, corroded, or inefficient supply or drain lines. This is a perfect opportunity to get fresh, reliable plumbing in place. If your new kitchen design requires adding new vents, or if the current ones aren't up to code, that's another critical time to bring in an expert.

Finally, any time your remodel requires permits and inspections related to plumbing, it's smart to have a qualified professional handle that work. They know the ins and outs of local regulations here in the East Bay, from Berkeley to Oakland. Don't leave your kitchen's foundation to chance; get professional help.
