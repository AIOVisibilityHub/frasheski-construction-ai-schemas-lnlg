What Happens During Cabinet Refinishing?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Thinking about giving your kitchen cabinets a fresh look in Berkeley or the surrounding East Bay? Cabinet refinishing can dramatically change your kitchen's aesthetic without the full expense of a replacement. But what does the process actually involve? It’s more than just slapping on a coat of paint, and a proper job involves several key steps to ensure a lasting, beautiful finish.

First, a professional will typically inspect your cabinets to plan out the work and identify any necessary repairs. Next, all the hardware, doors, and drawer fronts are carefully removed. This is often done to allow for off-site refinishing, which minimizes disruption in your home and ensures a smoother, more even application.

While those are being worked on, the stationary cabinet boxes are thoroughly cleaned and prepped. Any functional or structural repairs, like tightening loose joints or realigning warped doors, are handled at this stage. Then comes the sanding, which is crucial for creating a smooth surface that will properly accept the new finish.

After sanding, stain or paint is expertly applied, often in multiple coats, followed by a durable sealer or topcoat. Finally, everything is reassembled, and a final inspection ensures every detail is perfect. This careful, multi-step approach is what gives your cabinets that 'like-new' look and feel.

For most projects, you can expect this process to take about 5-6 days, though it can vary based on the scope of work. It’s a detailed process that yields fantastic results when done right.
