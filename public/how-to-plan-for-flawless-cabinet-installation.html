How to Plan for Flawless Cabinet Installation

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Planning for cabinet installation is all about precision and coordination. It’s not just about picking out beautiful cabinets; it’s making sure they fit perfectly and work seamlessly with the rest of your kitchen or bathroom. Here's a quick guide to a smooth process: First, confirm all cabinet dimensions, and seriously, double-check them against your space.

Second, verify your walls are plumb and your floors are level – this prevents major headaches later. Third, pay close attention to appliance openings to ensure everything has its spot. Fourth, identify all utility locations for plumbing, electrical, and HVAC rough-ins.

This is critical for avoiding conflicts. And finally, coordinate with other trades before installation; cabinets typically go in after rough-ins but before final finishes. Inaccurate measurements and poor sequencing are huge causes of delays.
