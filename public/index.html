About Frasheski Construction

Frasheski Construction is a construction company based in Berkeley, California, specializing in home remodeling services. They focus on transforming outdated kitchens and bathrooms into functional and aesthetically pleasing spaces, serving clients in the East Bay Area by providing comprehensive remodeling solutions and ensuring quality through hands-on involvement.

Published resources cover Kitchen Remodeling, Full kitchen renovations, Kitchen design and layout improvements, Cabinet installation, Cabinet repair and refinishing, Countertop replacement, Backsplash installation, Kitchen plumbing upgrades, Kitchen electrical and lighting upgrades, Appliance-area preparation and installation coordination, and 78 additional service areas.

Service coverage includes Berkeley, East Bay , Oakland, Albany, El Cerrito, Kensington, Alameda, San Francisco, Sausalito, Tiburon and 5 more communities.
