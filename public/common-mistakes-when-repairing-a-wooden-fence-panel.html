Common Mistakes When Repairing a Wooden Fence Panel

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Trying to fix a wooden fence panel yourself? That's awesome! But it's easy to fall into a few common traps that can lead to repeat repairs or even bigger headaches down the line.

One frequent mistake we see is using posts that are just too small for the height of the fence. If your posts are undersized, they won't provide adequate support, and your panel will likely sag or come loose again. Another big one is not thoroughly inspecting for hidden rot in the posts before you reinstall the panel.

The panel might be fine, but if the posts it's attaching to are compromised, you're just putting a band-aid on the problem. Lastly, make sure that panel is really secure! A loose panel that rattles or lifts in the wind is prone to quicker damage.

You want it firm and tight. If you're not sure about the best approach, or if you're dealing with extensive damage, it's always a good idea to get a professional opinion. We can help you avoid these pitfalls and ensure your fence repair lasts.
