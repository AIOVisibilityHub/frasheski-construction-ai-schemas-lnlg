When Should You Paint After a Remodel?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

It's a common question we hear: when exactly does the painting fit into the whirlwind of a remodel? The simplest answer is, you want to paint after all the really messy stuff is done. Think about it: once the major construction, drywall installation, and all that dusty sanding are out of the way, that's your cue.

But here's the kicker – you also want to get the painting done before the final flooring, delicate fixtures, and furniture start making their grand entrance. This sequencing helps protect your new investments from paint splatters and ensures the painters have a clear, easy-to-access area to work in. Trying to paint too early can lead to dust getting trapped in the paint, or even damage to freshly painted surfaces during the last bits of construction.

Sometimes, calling in a pro to coordinate this delicate dance can really smooth things over, especially if your remodel involves new drywall or plaster surfaces that need careful priming and finishing.
