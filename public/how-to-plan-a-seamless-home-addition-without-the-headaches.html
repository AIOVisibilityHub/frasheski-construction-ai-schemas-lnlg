How to Plan a Seamless Home Addition Without the Headaches

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Planning a home addition doesn't have to be a major source of stress. The key is to break it down into manageable steps. First, confirm what you want to achieve and whether it's truly feasible with your current home and lot.

Next, work with a skilled designer to develop detailed plans that account for everything, from the foundation up. It’s super important to check for permits and zoning requirements early on—don't wait until you're ready to build! Once plans are solid and approvals are in, you'll move through foundation work, framing, roofing, and all the interior finishes.

Remember, coordinating all these elements is a big job. A skilled contractor, like us at Frasheski Construction, can manage all these moving parts, ensuring a smoother journey from concept to completion. We’re well-versed in handling projects across the East Bay Area.
