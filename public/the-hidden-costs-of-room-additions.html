The Hidden Costs of Room Additions

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Dreaming of a new room? Room additions are exciting, but it's easy to overlook some hidden costs that can pop up. Beyond the obvious construction expenses, there are a few things you should definitely budget for. One big one is unexpected structural or utility issues. When you start opening up walls or digging foundations, you might uncover old plumbing, electrical, or structural problems that need fixing. These weren't in the original plan, but they're essential for safety and function.

Another cost often missed is permit and approval fees. Navigating the permit process itself can be time-consuming, and there are fees associated with obtaining those permits and scheduling inspections. Don't forget allowances and materials not included in the initial quote. Sometimes, a contractor's bid might exclude certain finishes or fixtures, expecting you to pick them out. Make sure you clarify exactly what's included.

Also, consider the impact on your existing systems. Will your HVAC system need upgrading to handle the new square footage? What about your electrical panel? These can add significant costs. To avoid these budget surprises, work with a contractor who provides a truly itemized bid and discusses potential hidden issues upfront.
